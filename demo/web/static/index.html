Your assistant thinks in the cloud. Your gate decides what leaves.

An AI assistant reads your email, so anyone who can email you can try to give it orders. Every email or payment it tries to send stops at a gate: a second model reviews it, you say yes or no, and a receipt is written before anything happens.
