homestead

A personal assistant that works while you sleep and can't be talked into spending your money.

It wakes on a schedule, reads only your new mail, uses skills you wrote, and pays only people you saved in its memory. Everything it tries to send stops at a gate: a second model reviews it, you say yes or no, and a receipt is written before anything happens. The cloud does the thinking. Your machine has the veto.
